# Village life and farm progression

Four processing machines turn milk into cheese, eggs into mayonnaise, wool into yarn, and carrots into pickles. Batches take one or two nights, accept normal/premium ingredients, preserve premium quality, and cannot be collected twice. Machines appear beside the house and produce goods for sale and rotating delivery requests. Pickles unlock at 20 friendship with Mali; Ton offers 10% machine discounts at 40 friendship.

Mali, Phupha and Ton have daily conversations, favorite gifts, seasonal dialogue, birthdays and friendship levels. Phupha gives three feed bags per week at 40 friendship. Gifts/talks cannot be repeated for more rewards on the same day. Birthday gifts grant +20 instead of +8 friendship.

A seven-day seasonal calendar shows weather, birthdays and four seasonal festivals on day six: Blossom Market, Valley Fishing Day, Harvest Festival, Animal Appreciation Day. Each has a real contribution/activity requirement, one reward per festival day, and decorations in the village. Activities can be joined from the calendar or the festival banner. Rewards are 250 money and +5 friendship with each villager.

The garden beside home can be decorated with wooden fences, lanterns, path stones and flower pots. Choose a position on a plan or use keyboard/Xbox focus to adjust coordinates and quarter-turn rotation. Existing objects can be moved free or sold at half price. Placement is limited to a clear garden area, prevents overlap and caps at 60 objects. Models have collision where appropriate; lanterns light at night without shadows.

Three independent save slots preserve the old save as slot 1. Saves validate before writing and use a temporary file plus one previous backup. Invalid main saves recover from backup. JSON export/import works through a browser file picker/download or paste; desktop uses file dialogs. Imports validate structure/checksum and ask before replacing a slot. Browser saves stay local to each origin; export to move between localhost and GitHub Pages. This is not cloud sync.

Fishing now has a shorter bite window, early-hook failure, species-specific movement, a slightly shrinking target, and visible surges requiring line relief. Long misalignment can let fish escape. Mouse, keyboard and Xbox use the same mechanics. Simulated tracking confirms perch/trout/carp remain catchable.

Administrator password updated at the owner's request; only its digest is included. Private access notes remain outside public exports and source archives.
