# Animal activities and everyday details

Eight activities: egg collection, duck guiding, cow milking, goat feeding, sheep shearing, pig scent hunt, dog fetch and cat teaser. Young livestock receive petting/brushing instead of production activities. All animals can also be petted. Activities support click targets/buttons, keyboard arrows/WASD/Space and Xbox D-pad/stick/A/B. Prompts automatically show original vector keyboard caps or controller circles.

Completing an activity earns +6 bond once per animal per game day; repeated play is allowed without extra bond. Adult chickens/cows/sheep collect existing products when completing their production activities; nothing is produced from an empty stock. Quick collection remains available. Cancelling does not award anything. Time and walking pause in activities.

Skippable 4-second sleeping scene; skip still advances exactly one day. Sun and moon positions now drive their respective directional lights. Wet soil resets at the beginning of each day. Creator surname corrected to คงเอม.

Administrator tools use a hidden keyboard shortcut and randomly generated password. Only a SHA-256 digest is included in the game. Five failed attempts pause authentication for 30 seconds; returning to the title screen locks the tools. Admin actions validate item/species keys and limit spawn/inventory sizes. This is a convenience lock on a client-side game, not server-enforced authorization.

The private password file is kept outside the Godot project and export/archive; do not distribute it.

## Web UI and fishing follow-up
Bundled Latin and symbol fallback fonts; HUD guide now has explicit full width and no wrapping, and no longer overlaps the tool bar. Milk timing band gets narrower and changes position each successful beat.

A timber fishing deck and ramp provide a real collision floor. Old saves with feet below bridge/deck surfaces are raised to the visible walking surface. Fishing spot is marked at the lake; the journal can set its waypoint. Cast, wait for a bite, hook, and hold/release to manage line tension. Perch, trout and carp go into the inventory and can be sold. Keyboard, mouse and Xbox controls supported.

## Seasons, weather and villager requests
Seasons remain seven days long. Autumn foliage turns gold, winter ground/roofs/leaves accumulate snow, and spring snow melts. Local rain, snow and falling-leaf particles follow the player and stop emitting under roofs. Evergreen trees retain their base color in autumn. Weather softens directional sunlight.

Daily weather is deterministic and saved games keep the same forecast. Rain automatically waters tilled plots; other days still reset wet soil. The journal shows today's weather and tomorrow's forecast, plus two rotating delivery requests. Rewards consume the required inventory once; completed requests cannot be claimed twice. Requests expire without penalties, and existing saves migrate automatically.

Sleep captions now inherit the bundled Thai font, NPC greetings use one action badge with no duplicate prompt, and the barn gateway has connected rail endpoints.
